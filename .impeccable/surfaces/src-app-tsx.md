# Surface: Denslab landing page (/)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: UMKM owners on phone/laptop. Action: open the Google Form to book or ask. Proof: authored mockups labelled "contoh", no invented numbers. Built in stages for owner review: (1) shell + first viewport, (2) services + process, (3) portfolio + FAQ + footer, (4) deploy + finish review + DESIGN.md.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The landing page is a studio "app": one big white console framed on light gray, laid out like the owner's Frogsound reference (rail, hero card, side panels, now-playing bar), so the services read as channels you can switch, not as a row of equal icon cards.
OWN-WORLD: Light gray ground #E3E3E6, frame #F3F3F4, white panels r=28px. Saturated fields own regions: sun yellow #FFDD33 hero, orange-red #F2541B promo panel, ink #17171A now-playing and buttons, periwinkle #8FB3F5 tile. Figtree with light/bold weight contrast in display. Round icon chips, pill buttons, ink play-button circles.
STORY: Visitor sees what Denslab makes (4 services), sees a project "playing" (progress of a sample job), picks a service and opens the booking form.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Desktop: framed console. Top bar logo + pill nav + "Booking" ink button. Left icon rail of the 4 services. Center yellow hero: "Website, Desain, Data. Beres." light/bold mix, mockup collage, ink "Booking Konsultasi" CTA. Right: Layanan chips panel + orange "Otomasi data UMKM" panel. Bottom: "Sedang dikerjakan" bar (sample project, labelled contoh) + Recommended work thumbs. Mobile: stacked, sticky booking bar.
FORM: pinned by owner reference. seed: none (pinned) — "referensi warna dan layoutnya seperti foto yang saya upload saat ini".
SIGNATURE INTERACTION: rail/chips switch the hero to the chosen service (copy + mockup swap), booking link carries the service.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
