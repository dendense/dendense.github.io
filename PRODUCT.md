# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Vite + React + TypeScript + Tailwind CSS v4, components from Dens-UI (https://dendense.github.io/dens-ui), copied as source the shadcn way. Static build deployed to GitHub Pages as the user site `dendense.github.io` (served from the domain root). No backend, no user login.

## Users

Owners and staff of Indonesian UMKM (small and medium businesses) and small brands who need a website, graphic design, product/packaging design, or a better way to record their business data, and who arrive on a phone or laptop from a shared link, Instagram, or search. They want to know quickly what Denslab can make for them and how to start a conversation.

## Product Purpose

Denslab offers four services: website development, graphic design, product design, and data-recording automation for UMKM (replacing manual notes and scattered spreadsheets with forms, structured sheets and simple dashboards). The site is a single public landing page that explains the services and routes every booking and question to a Google Form. Success: a visitor understands the offer and submits the form.

## Positioning

One small studio covering the whole path a UMKM walks: how it looks (graphic and product design), where it lives online (website), and how it runs day to day (data automation). Pricing is discussed in consultation, not published.

## Operating Context

- Booking and questions go through Google Forms owned by the site owner's Google account; the site links out to the form (form URL to be supplied by the owner).
- Visitors read in Bahasa Indonesia.
- The previous repository content (a trade journal and portfolio pages) was removed by the owner's decision; git history keeps it.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site only: no login, no accounts, no server-side form handling.
- No published prices; every service ends in a consultation/booking action.
- The first consultation is free (confirmed by the owner).
- Service area: online for clients anywhere, plus in-person meetings in one city (city name still to be supplied; `CITY` in `src/config.ts`).
- Site is bilingual, English default, Indonesian available.
- Public contact: Instagram @deny_desu, email denyfarras@gmail.com (subject "QnA Denslab"), owner site https://dendense.vercel.app/.
- Open: the Google Form URL, the in-person city, real portfolio pieces.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: **Denslab**.
- Visual reference pinned by the owner: the "Frogsound" music-app UI cards (yellow, orange-red, near-black, periwinkle blue on light gray with large-radius white panels, dashboard-style bento layout). Reference images supplied in chat.
- Component library: Dens-UI.

## Evidence on Hand

No client list, testimonials, project counts, ratings or prices exist yet; none may be invented. Portfolio visuals are authored mockups built in code and labelled as examples until the owner supplies real work.

## Product Principles

1. Every section leads to the booking form; the visitor never hunts for how to start.
2. Speak to UMKM owners in plain Indonesian, not agency jargon.
3. Show the work, don't claim it: mockups and concrete deliverables over adjectives.
4. Honest by default: no fake numbers, logos or quotes.
